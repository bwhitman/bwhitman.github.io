Last year I told the world about [Tulip, a portable low-power Python computer for making things.](https://notes.variogram.com/2023/02/14/tulip/)



[Tulip Creative Computer](https://github.com/bwhitman/tulipcc/blob/main/README.md). It's a computer you can carry with you,
